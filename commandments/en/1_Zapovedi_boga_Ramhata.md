title: "Commandments of God Ramhat"
description: "The 33 Commandments of God Ramhat"
order: 1
status: published
---

1. Return to your roots, and you shall open the gates to the Divine World (Prav).

2. Do not enter other Worlds bringing your own laws, but observe the laws of those Worlds.

3. Observe the Laws of Great Ramha; be wise and strive for perfection.

4. Do not follow Darkness, but walk in the Light, for those who follow Darkness shall perish before their time.

5. Do not heed those who call upon you to follow Falsehood, but listen to those who proclaim Truth.

6. Do not heed those who declare that Life is finite, for they themselves know not what they proclaim.

7. Do not listen to those who say that your neighbor is your enemy, but heed those who say that your neighbor is your friend.

8. Journey through the many Worlds, gaining knowledge of them and perfecting your Spirit.

9. Seek the Wisdom of the One God (Rod), and you shall find the Divine Light upon your Path.

10. Do not entrust the Vedas to those who would turn their Wisdom toward evil and the destruction of all living things.

11. Do not create false commandments and laws that contradict the Divine Laws and Commandments, for you shall bring destruction upon yourselves and lead your descendants astray from the Path.

12. Do not seek to conquer other Worlds, for those who harbor such intentions may lose their own World.

13. Do not assume that many Earths are lifeless, for life may exist upon them that your eyes cannot see and your senses cannot perceive.

14. Do not use Vaitmans and Vaitmaras for evil or for conquering other Worlds. Use them instead for good and for exploring the Worlds, and you shall gain the friendship of their inhabitants.

15. Do not show aggression toward those who arrive from other Worlds, for by your behavior they shall judge all the inhabitants of your World.

16. Do not destroy your World or its Nature, for in doing so you shall destroy yourselves and lose your World.

17. Let no wife reject her first husband, lest the Heavenly Rod turn away from her.

18. Let a daughter born in a Sacred Year enter into a Family Union with her husband only during a Sacred Year.

19. Let no father lay a finger upon another woman while his wife carries his child beneath her heart.

20. Take no other wives besides your own at any time, except during times of great calamity, when the men of the Lineage perish defending their homes, their Land, and their Faith.

21. Do not use the Fash-Destroyer against your own World, for you shall destroy your living World and deprive your Lineage of its descendants.

22. Preserve the Memory of all the Ancestors of your Lineage, and you shall receive the favor of your Gods and Ancestors.

23. Honor the Wanderers, the messengers of the One God (Rod), and heed their Words and Wisdom.

24. Do not live by laws created by people to deprive you of your Freedom, but live according to the Laws of the One God.

25. Do not speak the name of the One God in vain, but utter it only during the Great Sacred Festivals.

26. Honor the Heavenly Gods, your Father, and your Mother, for they have given you Life. Your Father and Mother have given you Spirit and Body, while the Gods have bestowed upon you Soul and Conscience.

27. Do not speak ill of those who have departed from your World, for only good should be spoken of those who have passed away.

28. Do not condemn those who have strayed from the Path of Light, but guide them back to the Righteous Path, for they shall answer for their deeds before their Conscience and Rod.

29. Do not be ashamed of what has been bestowed upon you by your Nature and the One Rod, but be ashamed of ignorance and lack of knowledge.

30. Give honor to your Gods and the One Creator, and you shall find peace and tranquility in your Life, and the Gates Between Worlds shall be opened to you.

31. Build Temples throughout all Worlds and seek Divine Wisdom, for whoever fails to comprehend Divine Wisdom shall lose themselves.

32. Hold sacred all the festivals passed down to you by your Ancestors. Fill your Life with joy and good deeds, to the Glory of your Gods and Ancestors.

33. Observe My Commandments, which I have given unto you, and you shall receive My help throughout all time.
