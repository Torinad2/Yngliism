The Commandments of the Light Gods-Ramhat, Svarog, Perun, Lada the Divine Mother, Stribog, Odin, Ladaad, Chislobog, Dazhdbog, and other Heavenly Gods-as well as the Two Great Principles that help people follow all the Commandments: to honor the Light Gods and their Wise Ancestors as sacred, and to live according to Conscience.
Since ancient times, many clans have given special reverence to the Heavenly Gods who were closest to them in Spirit and tradition. According to the legends, these Gods came to the aid of people throughout the lands of Midgard-Earth, where various clans and peoples lived.
As ancient Scriptures and Traditions recount, in those distant times, humans and Heavenly Gods formed bonds of brotherhood and helped one another. The Heavenly Gods passed down their simple yet wise **Commandments** to their earthly brothers and descendants so that Happiness and Joy would never leave their clans and future generations.
There are many Commandments of the Light Gods, just as there are many Heavenly Gods who bestowed them. To follow all the Commandments given by the Wise Heavenly Gods in everyday life, a person should remember the Two Great Principles:

**"Honor the Light Gods and your Wise Ancestors as sacred, and live according to Conscience."**

According to religious tradition, in ancient times, the Commandments of the Supreme God Perun were inscribed in runes on golden plates in the _Santias of the Veda of Perun_ (the Book of Perun's Wisdom).
The Commandments of other Heavenly Gods, according to the same traditions, were also recorded in ancient times in the Sacred Santias and Haratyas.
