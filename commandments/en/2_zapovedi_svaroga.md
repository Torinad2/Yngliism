1. Honor one another. Let sons honor their Mothers and Fathers, and let husbands and wives live together in harmony.
2. A man shall be devoted to one wife alone; otherwise, he shall not know salvation.
3. Turn away from Falsehood and follow Truth. Honor your Kin and the Heavenly Kin.
4. Observe three days of the week: the third, seventh, and ninth. Honor the Great Festivals. Therefore, all people should fast on the third and seventh days. Whoever works on the ninth day shall gain no prosperity, whether through fortune or talent, even if the other days are without hardship. The seventh day is given to people, livestock, and fish for peace and bodily rest. Visit one another, care for one another, and when joy fills your hearts, sing hymns to the Gods.
5. Observe the Great Fast, from the burning of Marena until the wedding of Zhiva. During this time, beware of Falsehood. Restrain your stomach from excessive eating, your hands from taking what belongs to others, and your lips from speaking slander.
6. Honor eggs in remembrance of the Egg of Koschei, which our Dazhdbog broke, thereby causing the Great Flood.
7. Observe Holy Week. Just as our Dazhdbog suffered from his crucifixion until his salvation by the Swan-Zhiva, so shall you mourn with humility. Do not drink intoxicating beverages or speak offensive words.
8. Honor the Kupala Days. Remember Perun's victory, how he defeated the Beast Skiper, freed his sisters, and purified them in the waters of Iriy.
9. Honor Perun's Day. Remember how Perun sought Diva's hand in marriage on the fifth day, how he defeated Niy and cast the God Veles down from the Heavens. Remember also the wedding of Perun!
10. Honor Mother Lada and the Heavenly Rod, the Protectors of all Clans and descendants of the Heavenly Kin.
11. After your harvest, remember Zlatogorka. Also honor the Day of Volkh the Wise, son of Indra, the Bright Falcon.
12. Honor the Day of Mother Makosh, the Radiant Great Mother, the Heavenly Mother of God.
13. Honor the Day of Tarkh Dazhdbog and remember his marriage.
14. Honor the Great Ynglia and your Gods, who are the messengers of the One God Rod.
15. Respect the elderly and protect the young. Seek the Wisdom passed down to you by your Ancestors.
16. Live in harmony with other Clans and help them when they ask for assistance.
17. Be willing to sacrifice your life in defense of your Home, your Kin, your Holy Faith, and your Sacred Land.
18. Do not impose the Holy Faith upon others by force. Remember that the choice of Faith is a personal matter for every free person.
19. Honor PASKHET and remember the fifteen-year journey from Daaria to Rasseniya, and how, in the sixteenth year, our Ancestors praised the Heavenly Rod for their salvation from the Great Flood.
20. Live in harmony with Nature and do not destroy it, for Nature is the foundation of your Life and of all living beings.
21. Do not offer blood sacrifices upon the Alatyr. Do not anger your Gods, for they find it abhorrent to receive the innocent blood of God's creatures.
22. Defend your Temples and Sanctuaries with a strong hand. Give all possible assistance to Wanderers and Kapens, who preserve the Ancient Mystery, the Word of God, and the Word of Wisdom.
23. Do not consume food containing blood, lest you become like wild beasts and many illnesses take hold of you. Eat pure food that grows in your fields, forests, and gardens. Thus shall you gain great strength, the strength of Light, and diseases, suffering, and torment shall not overtake you.
24. Do not cut your fair hair, whether of different shades or streaked with gray, for otherwise you shall not comprehend Divine Wisdom and shall lose your health.
25. Fathers, raise your sons and daughters and teach them to live righteously. Instill in them diligence, respect for the young, and reverence for the elderly. Bless their lives with the Holy Faith and the Wisdom of the First Ancestors.
26. Do not boast of your strength before the weak merely to gain their praise or inspire fear. Instead, earn honor and strength through battles against your enemies.
27. Do not speak falsely against your neighbor. Guard your lips against slander.
28. Perform good deeds for the glory of the Heavenly Rod, your Great Ancestors, and your Sacred Land.
29. Treat others as they treat you, for every deed shall be measured by the same measure.
30. Give one-tenth of your wealth to the One God and one-hundredth to your leader and his warriors, so that they may protect your land.
31. Do not reject what is unknown or unexplained. Instead, strive to understand the unknown and explain the unexplained, for the Gods help those who seek the knowledge of Wisdom.
32. Do not take the life of your neighbor, for it was not you who gave it, but the Gods. Yet defend yourselves, your loved ones, and your lands against those who come with violence.
33. Do not accept offerings or rewards for deeds accomplished through the Gift of the Gods or for your good deeds. Otherwise, the Divine Gift bestowed upon you shall disappear, and no one shall speak of the good you have done.
