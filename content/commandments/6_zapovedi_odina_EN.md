1. May the Family Union be founded upon mutual Love, consent, and Wisdom, so that the spouses may create a strong family and continue their ancient Lineage.
2. The greatest and most priceless treasures in the World are the Sword, Conscience, and Wisdom. All else shall not bring prosperity to the Lineage.
3. Master all the arts and Ancient Wisdom preserved by your Lineages since the beginning, and also learn the arts and Ancient Wisdom of other peoples. Share knowledge wisely with those who are ready to receive it with respect and understanding.
4. Remember that even an eye is not too great a price for a sip from the fountain of Ancient Wisdom, for one who knows the Runes can see even without eyes.
5. Preserve the secrets of the Runes and pass on the Ancient Sacred Wisdom to those who are ready to use it for the good of people and Nature, rather than for harm.
6. Every deceased person shall depart to the Heavenly World upon a Fiery Ship or through the Great Funeral Pyre of Kroda, and their possessions shall be placed upon the Ship or the Kroda. For everyone must enter Valhalla with the possessions that accompanied them upon the funeral pyre and make use of what they themselves buried in the earth.
7. The ashes of the deceased shall be entrusted to the waters of a river or sea, or gathered into a vessel and buried in the earth. In memory of the Wise and Knowledgeable, a burial mound shall be raised, and in honor of worthy people, a Memorial Stone shall be erected.
8. At the beginning of Winter, bloodless offerings shall be made to the Ancient Gods in gratitude for the harvest received; in the middle of Winter, for the coming spring growth; and in the middle of Spring, for Victory.
9. Every worthy man of any ancient Lineage must know that Conscience and the Sword must be cherished and protected from childhood.
10. Do not leave deliberate evil without a fair judgment, for evil that receives no proper response may multiply. Strive for Justice, guided by Conscience, Wisdom, and righteous Law.
11. Be steadfast and resolute in protecting the lives, freedom, and peace of your loved ones. Do not allow cruelty or injustice, but follow the eternal Righteous Path to the Halls of Valhalla.
12. Do not allow, O people, the eternal fear of the unknown to enter your hearts, nor foolishness and ignorance to enter your thoughts and words.
13. Every worthy man of any ancient Lineage must know that four Great Powers contribute to the growth and prosperity of his Lineage: Pure Conscience, Ancient Faith, Courage, and Diligence.
14. Return goodness only with goodness, O people, and do not commit evil or unworthy deeds where you have been welcomed with kindness and an open Soul.
15. Do not respond to evil committed against you with further evil, but protect yourselves and your loved ones according to Conscience and Justice. Remember that goodness deserves gratitude, while evil requires wise and righteous resistance.
16. Know, O people, that every descendant of the Ancient Gods should possess Ancestral Faith, clear Reason, an inquisitive Mind, a loving and courageous Heart, a pure Conscience, strong Hands, and a steadfast Will.
