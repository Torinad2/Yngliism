1. May the Spiritual Family Union be sanctified by all the Elements and the Heavenly Gods.
2. Honor your Lineage and care for its future, passing down Wisdom, Love, and the good traditions of your Ancestors to your descendants.
3. May the Fire of your hearts and hearths never be extinguished, if you live according to your Conscience and do not follow influences that contradict it.
4. Do not defile with your mortal remains the Sacred Land of your Ancestors, which has been blessed by the Blood and Sweat of your Forefathers.
5. Ease the passing of your loved ones through the Great Krodas and spacious Funeral Boats, so that their Souls may rise with the smoke of the Kroda into the Purest Svarga, to join your Ancestors.
6. Honor the memory of your Ancestors with bloodless offerings, for whatever you offer to them, they shall receive.
7. Do not destroy your Souls in pursuit of earthly riches, for all the treasures of the World of Yav cannot create even the smallest blessing of the World of Prav.
8. Failure to observe the Commandments deprives a person of the help of their Gods and Ancestors, for all the Gods assist only those who follow the Commandments and the Two Great Principles.
9. Resolve all disputes between your Lineages through brotherly peace, and celebrate victories over enemies with a feast of triumph.
10. Before establishing your Family Union, honor your Parents, listen to their wise advice, and make your decisions according to Conscience and free will.
11. Do not go against your Pure Conscience and the wise laws of your Ancestors, for there is no peace for the Soul upon the winding paths of life.
12. Cherish your Parents and the Elders of your Lineages, for they preserve the Wisdom and memory of ancient generations.
13. Do not allow restless winds to fill your minds, thunderclouds to darken your words, or raging storms to arise in your hearts.
14. Remember that the wealth and prosperity of your Lineages lie in your children, whom you must raise in Love, Goodness, and Diligence.
15. Know that the meaning of life and the prosperity of your Lineages rest upon four ancient foundations: Faith, Conscience, Love, and Will.
16. Create according to the calling of your heart, for your heart is an inexhaustible source of inspiration and Divine Power.
