1. May those who have received the blessing of God Perun for their Family Union be free from both Rati and Fatta.
2. Whoever gives a part of their Soul to their children does not diminish their Soul, but multiplies it.
3. Whoever squanders Love shall lose it, but whoever radiates Love shall multiply it.
4. Know, O people of the ancient Lineages, that you must treat all living beings created upon Midgard-Earth with Love and Kindness.
5. Whoever comforts and warms an orphaned child performs a good deed, but whoever also provides shelter and teaches the child Diligence performs an even greater deed.
6. Support your Lineages and your Faith in times of great hardship, showing Loyalty, Wisdom, and Compassion. Do not abandon your loved ones in times of trouble, for mutual support strengthens the Lineage.
7. Let Conscience and the Laws of your Lineage be the measure of all your deeds.
8. The Ancient Wisdom preserved within your Lineages is a great heritage of your Ancestors. Protect it from distortion and pass it on to those who are ready to receive it with Respect and use it for the good of people and Nature.
9. Reject thoughts and deeds that lead to Evil, Destruction, and the Boundless Inferno, regardless of where they originate.
10. Honor the Ancient Vedas, and may the Word of the Vedas always live upon your lips.
11. Protect the lives of your Kin and your neighbors, and you shall receive help from your Highest Gods.
12. Whoever shields their child from creative and constructive deeds harms the Soul of their child.
13. Whoever indulges every whim of their child weakens the child's Great Spirit.
14. The Vedas are understood through the Living Word, for only the Living Word reveals the meaning hidden within the Vedas.
15. Do not destroy your Nature or the spiritual heritage of your Lineages, for these are two Great Treasures that help preserve Life, Wisdom, and the continuity of your ancient Lineages.
16. Remember, O descendants of the ancient Lineages, that a Family Union founded upon Love, mutual Respect, and the blessings of loved ones gains a strong spiritual foundation. Protect it from discord and misunderstanding.
