1. May those born in Ramhat bring Harmony to all children born during the rest of the Year.
2. Let those born in the Heavenly Hall of the Maiden not seek their destined partner among those born in the same Heavenly Hall of the Maiden.
3. Do not waste your time, for whoever loses even a small portion of time loses their Life piece by piece.
4. From sunrise to sunset, O people, devote yourselves to Creation, and from sunset to sunrise, take your rest.
5. No personal freedom or will can change the flow of the River of Time or one's duty to the Heavenly Kin and your Gods.
6. Know, O people of the ancient Lineages, that every living being has its own appointed course of Time. Therefore, do not take another life, for by disrupting the course of another being's lifetime, you alter the course of your own.
7. If every person strives toward a purpose in life, fulfilling their destiny in harmony with the flow of their lifetime, their Life shall acquire the Highest Spiritual Meaning.
8. Do not waste your time, but fill your Life with Holiness and Good Deeds for the glory of your Gods and Ancestors.
9. Live according to Conscience, O people of the ancient Lineages, and you shall find Peace within your Souls, and the Time of your Life shall be filled with Meaning.
10. Every event in Nature and in your Life occurs at the time appointed by the Gods and in its proper season. No matter how much you may deny it, that which is destined to happen shall surely come to pass.
