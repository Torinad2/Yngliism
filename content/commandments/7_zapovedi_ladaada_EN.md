1. May the Family Union, strengthened by the Ancient Faith of the Forefathers, be founded upon the great principle bearing the Spiritual Swastika as an ancient symbol of Harmony, Creation, and the Eternal Cycle of Life.
2. Know that Harmony flows into the opposing Heavenly Halls, except for the Swastika and Tri-Swastika Halls, for these Heavenly Halls give rise not to Harmony, but to Mutual Love and Fertility.
3. Preserve within your great and small Lineages the Ancient Wisdom of your Ancestors and the Heavenly Wisdom of your Light Gods.
4. The Highest Heavenly Truth becomes accessible and understandable only to those who strive toward the heights of Creation, advancing along the Golden Path of Spiritual and Soulful Perfection.
5. Protect, O people, the Highest Heavenly Truth from distortion and malicious use. Pass it on to those who seek Wisdom with a pure Heart and an open Soul, so that the Golden Path of Spiritual and Soulful Perfection shall not be hidden from those who seek it.
6. Pass down the Wisdom of Creative Creation, preserved by your Lineages, to your descendants. May this Wisdom be passed from Parents to children and from older generations to younger ones.
7. Preserve, O people, the beauty of your Native Land for the descendants of your ancient Lineages, enriching its beauty for the glory of your Gods and Ancestors.
8. Do not allow evil and unrighteous anger to enter your Souls, O people, for they shall darken your Souls and harden your hearts.
9. Do not heed the voices of those who claim that the Highest Truth and the Wisdom of the Light Gods have been lost. For they themselves do not know what they proclaim, since that which is united with your Souls and Hearts can never be lost.
10. Honor, O people, the continuation of your ancient Lineages, and raise your sons in Love, Wisdom, Honor, and Conscience, so that they may become worthy guardians of the spiritual heritage of their Ancestors.
11. Honor, O people, your daughters and raise them in Love, Wisdom, Honor, and Conscience, so that they may become worthy guardians of the spiritual heritage of their Ancestors.
