1. May the firstborn children of the Lineage, blessed by Svarog, enter into a Family Union when they have reached the Years of Wisdom.
2. Preserve faithfulness, respect, and love within the Family Union. If the paths of the spouses should part, let each preserve dignity, wisdom, and care for their children.
3. May every Mother cherish love for her children, and in times of great hardship, may she find support, wisdom, and peace of soul.
4. May every Mother put her Soul into all her deeds and create Peace, Harmony, and Joy within her Home.
5. May every daughter of God fulfill her purpose according to Conscience and free will, for the good of her family and her Lineage.
6. May only Harmony and Love reign within the Family Union, and may troubles, hardships, and losses never befall it.
7. Live, O people, in harmony with Nature, nurturing and enriching it rather than destroying it.
8. May every daughter of God accept her chosen husband in the Family Union and love and honor him as the Divine Protector of her Lineage.
9. May every son of God accept his chosen wife in the Family Union and love and honor her as the Divine Guardian of the Family Hearth and the one who continues his Lineage.
10. Preserve your Family Unions, sanctified by the Gods, in times of joy and in times of sorrow. May the Light Gods help you, and may your ancient Lineages grow and flourish.
11. Daughters of God, cherish your hair from childhood, for it is the beauty and honor of your Lineages. Braid it into beautiful plaits and adorn it with protective charms.
12. Awaken from your dreams with the Bright Sun, and let the Shining Stars guide you to rest.
13. May children remain close to their Parents in their old age, surrounding them with care and respect, just as their Parents cared for them in their youth.
14. If your noble Lineage has more than six daughters, it is good to establish kinship with a strong Lineage that has more than six sons. Such a blessed union of families shall strengthen your ancient and honorable Lineages.
15. The more children there are in your Lineages, the more Love, Joy, and Happiness shall dwell among you, for your children strengthen your Lineages and lead them toward greatness and prosperity.
16. Never forget, O descendants of the ancient Lineages, the Patron Gods of your Lineages and all your Ancestors, for the Patron Gods and your Wise Ancestors are the mighty roots of your Lineages, the source of their life and prosperity.
