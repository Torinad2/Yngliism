---

1. Honor your Parents and care for them in their old age, for just as you care for them, so shall your children care for you...
2. Preserve the memory of all the Ancestors of your Lineages, and your descendants shall remember you...
3. Protect the elderly and the young, fathers and mothers, sons and daughters, for they are your Kin, the wisdom and the flourishing of your peoples...
4. Raise your children to love their Sacred Land, so that they are not tempted by wonders from distant lands, but may themselves create wonders even greater and more beautiful, for the glory of their homeland.
5. Do not perform miracles for your own benefit, but perform them for the good of your Kin and the Heavenly Kin...
6. Help your neighbor in times of trouble, for when trouble comes to you, your neighbors shall help you in return...
7. Perform good deeds for the glory of your Kin and your Ancestors, and you shall receive the protection of your Light Gods...
8. Help with all your strength to build Temples and Sanctuaries. Preserve Divine Wisdom and Ancient Wisdom...
9. Wash your hands after your labors, for whoever does not wash their hands loses Divine Strength...
10. Purify yourselves in the waters of Iriy, which flow through the Sacred Land, so that you may cleanse your bodies and sanctify them with Divine Power.
11. Uphold upon your land the Heavenly Law given to you by your Light Gods...
12. Honor the Rusalka Days, O people, and observe the Divine Festivals...
13. Do not forget your Gods. Burn incense and fragrant offerings in their honor, and you shall receive their favor and mercy...
14. Do not wrong your neighbors. Live with them in peace and harmony...
15. Do not humiliate the dignity of others, and your own dignity shall not be humiliated...
16. Do not bring harm to people of other faiths, for the One Creator God reigns over all Lands... and over all Worlds...
17. Do not sell your land for gold or silver, for you shall bring curses upon yourselves, and there shall be no forgiveness for you throughout all your days...
18. Defend your land and stand against those who come upon it with weapons and evil intentions.
19. Protect the Lineages of the Rassens and Svyatoruses from armed enemies who come to your lands with malicious intent.
20. Do not boast of your strength when riding into battle, but speak of your deeds when returning from the battlefield...
21. Preserve Divine Wisdom and pass on the Sacred Wisdom to those who are ready to receive it with respect and understanding.
22. Do not attempt to persuade those who do not wish to listen to you or heed your words...
23. Protect your Temples and Sanctuaries from desecration and destruction. Preserve your Sacred Places and the Faith of your Ancestors so that your spiritual heritage shall not be lost.
24. Remember the land of your Ancestors and maintain your connection with your Kin wherever you may be. Honor your roots and pass their memory on to your descendants.
25. Do not rejoice in the sorrow of others, for whoever takes pleasure in another's suffering invites sorrow upon themselves...
26. Do not speak ill of or mock those who love you. Return love with love, and you shall receive the protection of your Gods... Love your neighbor if they are worthy of it...
27. Let no brother take his sister as his wife, nor a son his mother, for you shall anger the Gods and corrupt the blood of your Lineage...
28. Choose your wife through love, mutual respect, and consent, for the strength of the Family Union is determined by faithfulness, kindness, and purity of intention, not by ancestry or skin color.
29. Women, do not wear men's clothing, lest you lose your femininity. Wear instead what is proper for you...
30. Do not break the bonds of the Family Union sanctified by the Gods, for you shall go against the Law of the One Creator God and lose your happiness...
31. Let no child be killed in its mother's womb, for whoever takes the life of an unborn child shall bring upon themselves the wrath of the One Creator God...
32. Wives, love your husbands, for they are your protection and support, as well as the protection and support of your entire Kin...
33. Do not drink excessive amounts of intoxicating beverages. Practice moderation in drinking, for whoever drinks too much loses their human dignity...
